# Launch runbook — the complete experience, September 9, 2026

The app now automates everything it can. What remains is a short list of human actions, each of which is a credential, a Shopify or GHL setting, a recording, or a send. The live version of this list is the **Launch board** at `/studio` (owner sign-in): every row shows its state and the exact action that turns it green, without exposing any value.

## What the app does on its own once connected

- **Purchase → ticket.** A paid Shopify order arrives by webhook, is reconciled against the Admin API, and each paid line becomes an email-matched ticket in the same table as the imported Summit purchasers. The purchaser signs in with the purchase email, verifies it, and chooses **Activate my purchased lessons** at `/redeem`. No purchase code, no `ACADEMY_ACCESS_TERMS_JSON`. This applies to Shopify orders and to GHL-checkout orders alike. Any tier you give an approved rolling term in `ACADEMY_ACCESS_TERMS_JSON` switches to the purchase-code path instead, because a rolling clock has to start at activation; tiers without a term are open-ended tickets. Decide that per tier before launch. Activation is explicit on purpose: it starts any access period, and signing in or watching the free training does not (see `docs/email-ticket-activation.md`). Refunds and cancellations flip the ticket inactive on the next reconciliation. (`ACADEMY_EMAIL_TICKETS_ENABLED`)
- **Purchase → confirmation.** One transactional email per paid line to the purchase email, whether or not an account exists yet, plus SMS when the purchaser already has an account with SMS consent. It tells them to sign in with that email, verify it, and choose Activate. The sender re-reads the order and grant before every attempt. Keep `ACADEMY_ACCESS_EMAIL_ENABLED` off while this is on, or purchasers get two emails.
- **Sign-up → welcome.** Welcome email (and SMS with consent) through the GHL inbound workflow, every five minutes via pg_cron.
- **Learning → coaching.** SPINXP decides the reason and timing (drop-off, practice, stalled, feedback, approval); Thoth or AI Spin adds wording; local daytime and a daily cap apply. (`ACADEMY_LEARNING_NUDGES_ENABLED`)
- **Recordings → guides.** Vimeo slots play with watch telemetry; captions flow in through the Vimeo API when `VIMEO_ACCESS_TOKEN` is set; the guides cite timestamps.
- **Historical purchasers.** The imported tickets already in the database activate the same way: sign in with the purchase email, verify it, choose Activate. Nothing activates on sign-in alone.

## Human side, in order

1. **Shopify (15 minutes).** In the Shopify Dev Dashboard create an app that uses client credentials with `read_orders`, install it on the store, and add `SHOPIFY_CLIENT_ID` and `SHOPIFY_CLIENT_SECRET` in Lovable → Project Settings → Secrets (`ACADEMY_SHOPIFY_SHOP` is the store domain; a legacy custom-app token via `SHOPIFY_ADMIN_ACCESS_TOKEN` + `ACADEMY_SHOPIFY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` still works). Subscribe `orders/paid`, `orders/updated`, `orders/cancelled`, `refunds/create` to `https://aiautopilotsummit.com/api/public/webhooks/shopify`. Set `ACADEMY_SHOPIFY_ENABLED=true`, `ACADEMY_EMAIL_TICKETS_ENABLED=true`, and `ACADEMY_ACCELERATOR_ENDS_AT` (the programme end as an ISO timestamp with timezone, e.g. `2026-12-31T23:59:59-05:00`). Confirm the Accelerator product's checkout link is configured.
2. **Purchase-email verification (15 minutes).** This gate is what lets anyone activate a ticket, historical or new. In Supabase Auth: turn on Confirm email (no auto-confirm), set the production Site URL, allow the `/join` redirect, and change the **Confirm signup** and **Magic Link** templates to the `token_hash` link in `docs/email-ticket-activation.md`. Then set `ACADEMY_EMAIL_TICKET_LINKS_ENABLED=true`.
3. **GHL sender (20 minutes).** Pick one transport. Webhook (default): open the workflow behind `ACADEMY_GHL_WEBHOOK_URL`, verify the email action's sender, add an SMS action that runs only when `send_sms` is true, branch on `event_name` (`webinar_registered`, `purchase_confirmed`, `learning_*`, `webinar_not_started`) and use `message_subject`, `message_html`/`message_text` and `sms_text` from the payload. Direct API (`ACADEMY_GHL_TRANSPORT=api`): follow `docs/ghl-direct-messages.md` (private token, location, verified sender email, E.164 SMS number, receipt SQL). Either way, send a test to your own inbox and phone. If you sell through GHL checkout instead of Shopify, complete `docs/ghl-payments.md` and set `ACADEMY_CHECKOUT_PROVIDER=ghl`; the board swaps the blocking purchase row for you.
4. **Prove the loop (15 minutes).** Sign up with a fresh email and confirm it from the inbox link. Welcome email within 10 minutes. Then place a real $22 General Admission order with a second email, and confirm: purchase confirmation arrives, creating an account with that email and choosing Activate at `/redeem` unlocks Summit Day 1 and Day 2 with no code. Also test one existing account: Email me a verification link, then Activate.
5. **Recordings (as they are ready).** Paste Vimeo links into the seven lesson slots and each Accelerator day. Add `VIMEO_ACCESS_TOKEN` and turn on auto-captions on each upload so transcripts arrive on their own. Day 1 VIP and the Emerald intensive transcripts are already bundled.
6. **Welcome videos.** Record from the filming pack and paste into `VITE_ACADEMY_VSL_URL`, `VITE_ACADEMY_VSL_SUMMIT`, `VITE_ACADEMY_VSL_VAULT`, `VITE_ACADEMY_VSL_ACCELERATOR`.
7. **Guides.** `ACADEMY_BOOKING_URL` for the 1-on-1 calendar. For the live avatar: `LIVEAVATAR_API_KEY`, `LIVEAVATAR_AVATAR_ID`, `LIVEAVATAR_VOICE_ID`, `ACADEMY_AVATAR_ENABLED=true`, then run one session yourself. `ACADEMY_LEARNING_NUDGES_ENABLED=true` once the welcome email is verified; `ACADEMY_THOTH_MESSAGES_ENABLED=true` if you want AI-personalised wording.
8. **Blast.** From GHL or Mailchimp, send the launch email to registrants, purchasers and the waitlist with the join link. Purchasers get one instruction: create your account with your purchase email, verify it, then choose Activate my purchased lessons.
9. **Watch the board.** `/studio` shows every row. Green across the blocking rows means the complete experience is live.

## What is still unverified from inside the app

Inbox and phone delivery are only proven by your own test. The Supabase Auth email templates cannot be read from the app, so the verification gate is only proven by one fresh signup and one returning-account link in a real inbox. Shopify webhook subscriptions can only be confirmed in the Shopify admin or by the app once the credentials exist. The Vimeo caption pull and the live avatar were built from documentation and need one real run each.
