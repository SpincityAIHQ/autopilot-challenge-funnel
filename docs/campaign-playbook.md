No email, text, call, page, or video may use a different date, time, or promise.

## Non-negotiables

- No income promises, fake attendee counts, fake deadlines, or fake scarcity.
- No claims about partners or endorsements without written proof.
- Email, SMS, and AI-call consent are separate, unchecked by default, and never required to buy.
- SMS always honors STOP and HELP.
- AI/prerecorded calls require seller-specific written consent, an AI disclosure at the start, DNC checks, and quiet-hour controls.
- Suppress buyers from every sales sequence for a product as soon as verified fulfillment confirms they own it.
- Never keep selling a product the person already bought.
- Use the exact ticket-level structure in `docs/email-segmentation-map.md`.

### Monday, August 31

**Subject:** What should you build first?

Point them back to the 30-day build order. Ask them to choose one working system, not ten unfinished ideas.

**Subject:** A prompt is not a workflow

Explain the difference between a one-time answer and a repeatable work path with a clear start, handoffs, checks, approvals, and outputs.

**Subject:** The three places AI business builds usually break

2. What is working?
3. Where are you stuck?

## Suppression and hygiene

- A verified purchase removes the buyer from the sales sequence for that product.
- A VIP purchase stops VIP sales. A Vault purchase stops VIP and Vault sales. An Intensive purchase stops all funnel sales.
- A refund or dispute removes the buyer from post-purchase delivery for the reversed product and recalculates the highest active level.
- An unsubscribe stops marketing but never deletes the purchase record.
- Do not send more than one Summit marketing email per calendar day to one address. Transactional access and urgent event changes remain separate.
- Never send SMS or AI calls during prohibited local quiet hours.
